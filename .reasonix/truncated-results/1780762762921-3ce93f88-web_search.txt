  The Potion Workshop in the Spectrum mod allows players to create potions, tipped arrows, and fillable items like potion pendants [1]. Unlike vanilla brewing stands, this station utilizes a wide range of reagents to modify potion properties such as duration and potency [1]. Recipes are defined by the status effect identifier, a base duration in ticks, and a potency modifier [1]. Documentation for these recipes, including specific reagents and their effects, is maintained on the official Spectrum GitHub wiki [1][2]. # Custom Potion Workshop Brewing Recipes - DaFuqs/Spectrum GitHub Wiki
The Potion Workshop is Spectrums Way of creating various Potions and Tipped Arrows. Here the Potion Pendant can be filled with Effects, too. The main differences to the vanilla Brewing Stand are:
* Instead of Blaze Powder, crafting requires a Mermaids Gem per crafting cycle
* Brewing is done in a single step
* Players can choose from a wide range of Reagents to increase the properties of their Potions
* Cheaper Creation of Tipped Arrows, more similar to Bedrock Edition efficiency
![Potion Workshop Brewing](https://github.com/DaFuqs/Spectrum/raw/1.20.1-main/images/wiki/potion_workshop_brewing.png?raw=true)

## Overview

Spectrum is a progression and exploration-based magic mod where you combine colors to create magical tools and machines,
with the ultimate goal of discovering what lies beneath the once-impenetrable Bedrock floor.

In the process, you will gain a new perspective and knowledge of things that have always been around you. Things you
have always noticed as being out of place, but have never been able to put your finger on because you have lacked the
knowledge and tools to observe them. Now is the time!

Spectrum is progression-based and tries not to give everything away at first glance.
Your journey through its advancement trees will be cluttered by realizations, puzzles and lots of "aaaaaah"s.
Each step of your journey will be hinted at through advancements and the in-game guidebook you receive as soon as you
find your first geode.

There is no hand-holding, so everything will require a fair bit of experimentation or exploration.
This also means that it may not be the best fit for everyone. Everyone plays Minecraft differently and that is perfectly
fine!

**_Spectrum is not meant to be rushed or grinded through. It is best played alongside your playthrough._**

## Everything you ever wanted in a Magic Mod

- handcrafted progression & revelation system
- lots of new magical equipment, items, blocks, decorations
- lots of new enchantments & potion effects
- New fluids with full collision & unique effects
- build your own, customizable crafting structures
- lots of handcrafted new sounds and particles
- new unique ores, plants and geodes
- custom structures with intriguing puzzles

## Is this mod for me?

**You might enjoy Spectrum if you like the following:** Outer Wilds; Myst; Thaumcraft; Chromaticraft; figuring out how
and why things work

**You will probably hate Spectrum if you like the following:** Generic tech mod #7 with linear progression that tells
you exactly what to do at every step; following video tutorials 1:1

# Progressing through the mod

You may notice that most items only show up with a locked recipe in your recipe viewer.
In Spectrum, recipes and their matching guidebook entries are unlocked step by step, giving you something new to toy
with, with each new discovery you made.

Everything will be hinted at via advancements and the guidebook gifted to you when you stumble upon your first geode:
your own, personal notebook.
Everything you stumble upon, every recipe you unlock, every discovery you make you will note there.

![Guidebook](/images/readme/guidebook.png)

Spectrum features lots of new ways to express yourself.
Its multiblock structures are designed in a way to feel satisfying, fun to play with and to give you the opportunity to
express your creativity by giving you lots of freedom on how to construct and personalize them.

# Lots and lots of revamped models & textures
Believe me, @Azzy outdid herself with this one.
There have been improvements all over the place, with new models for Pigment Pedestal, Enchanter, the Colored Trees, lots of equipment and so much more.
Special thanks to @Noaan for getting the new Bedrock Armor model implemented code-wise and @Jas for doing all the color variations.
On that note: You can find the old textures activating the "Programmer Art" texture packs. Even there has been some work. @Jenny77 made some programmer art, that looks kinda nice, actually. Check it out!
# Energy System
At the start of midgame, the player now unlocks the Color Picker, which is able to convert Pigment to Ink.
Ink comes in all 16 Colors and can be stored in a range of items to power your equipment. For example, Ink can be used to power the Nature's/Radiance Staff, additionally to Sparklestone/Vegetal.
Most of the Trinkets will be changed to require Ink, but this will come later, until existing players had the chance to catch up with this update and get their hands on some Ink. Currently the use of Ink is still pretty sparse, but a lot is in the works.
Note: With "Ink" resembling a fluid, it very much behaves like one when transferring.
# The Paintbrush
A new tool for your arsenal as an Ink Artist. Your main working tool will gradually unlock more and more abilities as you progress.
For now, aspiring artists are able to color blocks in-world using Pigment and fling colored projectiles around. I added a few tricks to make flinging your Ink extra satisfying, of course <3
Crouch-Use to bring up the color selection.
This toolset will gradually improved upon as the magic and spell building system get's added.
You can also hit your Pigment Pedestal with it to start crafting, as an alternative for Redstone. (it still seems to react to redstone)
# Boostable Trinkets
These aren't your mundane trinkets. They will get more powerful the more Ink you pump into them.
This includes a new Trinket Slot: Gloves!
# The Cinderhearth
An Ink-powered blast furnace that can be enhanced with upgrades to smelt blazingly fast, double smelting output, grant lots of XP or consume less Ink.
It only comes with 2 positions slots. Which ones will you choose?
For modpack makers: The Cinderhearth recipe type supports more than one output per input, including chance based output.

## Overview
Spectrum is a colour-themed magic mod all about discovery and exploration. You will combine the sixteen colours and magical resources you discover on your journey to create magical tools and machines. The ultimate goal is to discover what lies beneath the once impenetrable bedrock floor.
In the process, you will gain a new perspective on things that have always been around you. You will notice things that have always seemed out of place, but you have never been able to identify them because you lacked the knowledge and tools to observe them. Now is the time!
Spectrum is progression-based and doesn't give everything away at first glance. As you progress through its advancement trees, you will have many realizations and solve lots of puzzles. Each step of your journey will be hinted at through advancements and the in-game guidebook that you will receive as soon as you find your first geode.
